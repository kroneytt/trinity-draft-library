TRINITY DRAFT Glossary
Card Library Rulebook

Glossary & Terminology

A comprehensive guide to keywords, auras, zones, and standard terminology used in Trinity Draft.

keywords

On Entry TIMING
Triggers immediately when this card is placed onto the battlefield.
On Attack TIMING
Triggers when this unit initiates an attack.
Trigger TIMING
Triggers automatically when the stated condition is met.
Activate TIMING
Can be activated by the player during their turn by paying the indicated cost.
Continuous TIMING
A passive effect active as long as this card remains in the specified zone.
On Destroy TIMING
Triggers when this card is destroyed and sent to the graveyard.
Shadow Condition TIMING
Timing condition required to play this card from the Shadow Zone.

auras

Quick Aura
This unit can attack on the turn it enters the battlefield.
Bad Aura
When this unit loses a battle, destroy the opponent's battling unit.
Sky Aura
This unit cannot be blocked by units without Sky Aura.
Steel Aura
Attacks hit the Life Deck directly, ignoring Walls.

summons

Shinra Summon
Alternative summon: stack on top of battlefield units whose total cost matches exactly.
ZERO Summon
Alternative summon: cost becomes 0 and loses Color Condition when specified requirements are met.
Reduced Summon
Summon cost is reduced according to the specified condition.

costs

Bloody Drive
Take damage from a chosen Favorable Wall as an activation cost.
Soul Flare
Send cards from this unit's Soul to the graveyard as an activation cost.
Counter Flare
Remove Trinity Counters from your Reserve as an activation cost.
Counter Vortex
Remove 10 Trinity Counters from your Reserve to unleash a massive effect.
Abyss Gate
Place cards from your graveyard into this unit's Soul as an activation cost.
Alchemy
Destroy an Equip or Set card on your battlefield to activate.
Combine
Place specified battlefield cards into this unit's Soul to transform its stats.

thresholds

Overdrive
Condition met if you have at least the specified amount of Acquired Life.
Overcount
Condition met if you have at least the specified amount of Trinity Counters in your Reserve.
Requiem
Condition met if at least the specified number of units were sent to the graveyard from the battlefield this turn.

special

Oni Charge
Triggers when this card is placed into the Color Zone for Color Charge.
Jigoku Back
Triggers when this card is sent directly from your deck to the graveyard, allowing it to enter the battlefield without paying cost.
Color Shift
When paying a summon cost, you may send this unit to the graveyard to generate the indicated basic colors.
Adjustment
Look at the top X cards of your deck and place them on the top or bottom of your deck in any order.
Burst
Effect triggered when this card is revealed during a Burst Check from the Life Deck.
Epic Rule
At game start, choose 1 from outside the game into hand. Returns to deck instead of graveyard.
